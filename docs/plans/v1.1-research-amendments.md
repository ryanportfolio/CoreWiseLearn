## Lane 1: engine, input, storage, PWA

Files: `src/engine/**`, `src/app/**`, `vite.config.ts`, `index.html`, `public/config.json` (new), `scripts/check-precache.mjs` (new).

- Loop `maxFrameTime` to 0.1 s (2).
- Runtime resolution scale: start at DPR up to 1.5, cap backing pixels near 1.5 M, step 1.0 / 0.85 / 0.7 when p95 work time stays high for 2 s, with hysteresis (46). This replaces the fixed `MAX_DPR = 1`.
- Service worker: `registerType: 'prompt'`, apply the update on the hub after a celebration, never mid-round; wrap lazy loads in a reload-once catch (2, 48).
- Save: back up the raw blob before migrating, never replace unmigratable data, read-only when the stored version is newer; prefix keys `cwl.v1.`; never `localStorage.clear()`; `navigator.storage.persist()` after the first finished round; save on every award and on `visibilitychange` (2, 48).
- Input: act on `pointerdown` with no button filter; skip `event.repeat`; cancel Ctrl-wheel and Ctrl plus, minus, 0; swept pointer segment per frame exposed to scenes (4, 5, 8).
- Manifest `display_override: ["fullscreen"]`, scripted `requestFullscreen()` on first gesture, `navigator.keyboard.lock()` attempt (8). README steps for Sticky Keys and touchpad gestures.
- `public/config.json`: `uiScale`, `masterTrimDb`, `motionScale` override, `keyboardLayout` (abc or qwerty), seeded `profiles` (ships empty), `playtestLog: false` (9, 31, 33, 39, 44, 51). Loaded once at boot into `AppServices.config`.
- `?debug` hooks: forced tier, RNG seed, time scale, frame-time overlay, resolution-scale key (50).
- `scripts/check-precache.mjs` fails when a `public/` file is missing from the service worker manifest; payload target under 5 MB for the full precache (47).
- Audio graph: master gain into `DynamicsCompressorNode` into a `WaveShaper` soft clip near -3 dBFS; mute ramps gain; three-state mute icon (on, muted, waiting); capture-phase unlock on pointer and key events from boot; sounds made while suspended are dropped (36, 37).

## Lane 2: Bubble Pop redesign

Files: `src/games/bubble-pop/**`, `src/dev/bubble-pop.ts`.

- Pop on cursor entry at every tier; any button or key pops the nearest bubble within 1.5x its radius; swept hit test from the engine (3, 4).
- No decoys. Every bubble is a valid pop (10).
- Tier table: rise speed 45 to 60 / 70 to 100 / 110 to 140 px/s, live cap 2 to 4 / 4 to 6 / 6 to 8, spacing 1.5 / 1.2 / 1.0 diameters, round length 60 / 75 / 90 s, drawn size 128 to 144 px, bubbles never time out, tier 1 escapes recycle and are not misses (11).
- Hit radius = drawn radius x 1.25 / 1.15 / 1.10 by tier, set at round start, hit diameter never under 96 px (12).
- Adaptive controller: motor pop rate targets 85 percent (first 3 to 5 s and idle stretches excluded); tier down under 75 percent for a round, up at 90 percent over 30+ bubbles two rounds running; one step per round boundary; sessions start one step below the stored tier; in-round only the spawn gap and a drift-toward-cursor assist move. Separate counting estimator sets the count range on 60 / 65 to 80 / 80 percent bands (13).
- New name: first round untimed, ends after about 8 pops with 3 stars; first bubble spawns within 250 px of the pointer (14).
- Round shape: 10 s warm-up, 3 to 4 waves of 8 to 12 s with 4 to 6 s breathers, finale shower (15).
- Counting: each pop flies a theme icon into a jar of 10; numeral beside dots at 3, 5 and 10; round-end tally lands item by item; quotas derived from the spawn schedule and asserted completable in dev (16).
- Pitch: pop n plays step n of the pentatonic ladder; bubble size sets the noise-body filter; pops play on the input frame (17).
- Combo: 2.5 s window, milestones 3, 6, 10, capped at 10, shown as pips or an arc, broken chain shrinks silently (18).
- Juice limits: no shake, no hit-stop on pops; squash 80 to 120 ms; 6 to 10 particles per pop, about 150 live; flash rule (19).
- One surprise spawn in about 15 (20).
- Stars: 1 for finishing, 2 for beating the child's own median longest combo over recent rounds, 3 for that plus a completed 10-count; aim for 60 to 70 percent of rounds at 3 stars (21).
- Misses: silent or a quiet bloop; mascot never droops (22).
- Celebration about 4 s, skippable from the first frame, 4 variants with no back-to-back repeat, then a still rest screen with equal Again and Home and a 250 to 400 ms input guard (23).
- Sticker: one per finished round, drawn without replacement from the current page, child picks one of two wrapped gifts, flies into the book; silhouettes only inside the book (24).

## Lane 3: name entry, hub, sticker book, nudge

Files: `src/scenes/**`, `src/ui/**`, `src/dev/*`.

- Name cap 10; one fixed pentatonic note per letter; skip repeats; merge presses within 30 ms; at most about 8 sounds per second; digits burst that many sparkles; any other key sparkles (28, 29).
- Commit: pulsing play arrow after the first letter; Enter or Space commits; letters bounce in order with the name tune; letter count shown as numeral beside dots (30).
- On-screen keyboard: ABC rows of 9, 9, 8 by default, QWERTY from config; light the matching key on every physical press; invisible padding fills gaps (31).
- Returning-child picker: always one click, never auto-resume; bubbles show initial, critter and colour assigned at creation and forced unique; prefix filter on typed letters; ignore a second click within 400 ms; at most about 5 profiles (32).
- Name matching: NFKC, lowercase, strip non-letters, collapse runs of 3+; exact match is the same child; edit-distance-1 "is this you?" bubble for names of 4+ letters, never an automatic merge; seeded profiles with aliases from config (33).
- No-typing path: a big "surprise animal" bubble starts play without a name (34).
- Hub tiles, name bubbles and keys highlight on hover and act only on click or a key while hovered (7).
- Native OS cursor stays; a halo at the true hit radius; the cursor-following character is a trailing buddy that never owns the hit test; demos move that character (6).
- Sticker book: 3 pages of 8 for v1; extras upgrade owned stickers; each sticker plays a 2 to 4 s animation when clicked (24).
- Break nudge: visible play time only, at the next round boundary, dusk colours, a content yawn, replay stays visible at 96 px or more (25).
- Reduced motion into `motionScale` with the config override (44).
- Mascot spec: 1:2 head to body, four states (idle smile, delight, curious, yawn), feeling through squash and bounce, never sad (43).

## Lane 4: typeface, style bible, art rules, audio loudness

Files: `public/fonts/**`, `docs/style-bible.md`, `src/ui/draw.ts` font constant, `public/art/**` touch-ups, `src/audio/**` trim.

- Self-host Andika (OFL), uppercase only, glyphs 56 px or more, atlas baked after `document.fonts.load()` resolves (35).
- `docs/style-bible.md`: corner radius, outline weight, palette tokens, mascot and eye spec, shadow rule, icon set, one line per game naming its medium (41).
- Bubble and colour rules: solid bubbles with tinted rim and flat highlight; 3:1 contrast against backgrounds checked by a script; every colour paired with a shape; grayscale check (42).
- AI art limited to reviewed stills; build script trims, alpha-bleeds, resizes and palette-snaps; log prompt, model and date per asset (40).
- `masterTrimDb` honoured from config; music starts about 12 dB under SFX peaks (39).
