# Future worlds

Each profile owns its collections, progress, creations, and future world. Bubble Bay remains the only playable game in v1.1. The following are approved directions for later games.

- **Dinosaur Playground** uses clay-like characters for hatching, bouncing, splashing, and discoveries. **Dino Picnic** can practice quantities and comparing more and less.
- **Little Train Town** uses painted wooden toys. Building routes is creative play; **Letter Train** can match letters, pair uppercase and lowercase, then form short words and the child's name.
- **Rainbow Workshop** uses layered paper shapes to create pictures and creatures. **Shape Workshop** can match identical and rotated shapes and connect spoken names to shapes. **Word Garden** can connect spoken and printed words to visible transformations.
- **Spider-Man's Web Playground** uses a friendly toy-scale city with red-and-blue comic accents. Spider-Man is the nephew's favorite. Possible games include assisted web swinging, catching floating shapes in webs, and connecting numbered web points. Rescues have gentle goals and no injury, frightening enemies, or countdown pressure. This world stays available to every profile; each child's creations and progress remain separate.

Learning difficulty and control difficulty are separate. A game records evidence of understanding only when its action asks for that understanding. Bubble popping does not measure number knowledge. No activity requires reading to navigate. Short spoken clips accompany the first deliberate literacy game.

Creative games save on leaving and do not need a round, stars, or forced completion. Round games celebrate attainable accomplishments. The world stays as the child left it: no streak, decay, hunger, or absence penalty.

## First child playtest

Watch each child separately before explaining anything. Note whether they can choose their profile, discover popping, recover after an idle pause, leave a round, and find their sticker. Watch whether sounds invite repetition and celebrations are welcomed or skipped. Ask which creature they want to see again. These observations guide tuning; the app collects no analytics.
