# Proposed amendments to the v1 build prompt

Date: 2026-10-02. Each item names a change to the build prompt and a one-line rationale with its source section. The prompt is not rewritten here; the owner applies what they accept. Where sections disagree, the call recorded in [README.md](README.md#where-the-sections-disagree) is used.

## Amendments

### Context and scaffold

1. **Update the context line.** Replace "essentially empty" with: the branch already holds a playable v1 commit (engine, name entry, hub, sticker book, bubble pop) and uncommitted work; review and fix it rather than scaffold from scratch. Rationale: section 06 audited the existing `src/engine/` and built its defect list on it ([06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
2. **Fix four scaffold defects first.** Loop `maxFrameTime` 0.25 s to 0.05 to 0.1 s; `MAX_DPR` 1 to 1.5; service worker `autoUpdate` to `prompt` mode applied silently on the hub; save migration backs up the raw blob, never replaces unmigratable data, and goes read-only when the stored version is newer. Rationale: each can cause a stutter spiral, blurry art, a reload mid-round or a wiped sticker book ([06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).

### Input

3. **Pop on entry at every tier, not hover only at the easiest.** Replace "click or hover-to-pop at easiest tier" with: a bubble pops when the cursor first enters its hit area at every tier; any mouse button or any key pops the nearest bubble within 1.5x its radius. Rationale: children hover then slip off during the click, and the study authors suggest crossing activation ([01](01-design-principles.md), [02](02-pop-game-feel.md)).
4. **Add a swept hit test.** Test the segment from last frame's pointer position to this frame's against each bubble, using two scalars. Rationale: at 20 fps a fast sweep jumps 150 px and skips a 128 px bubble ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
5. **Make every button the same.** Act on `pointerdown` with no button filter, cancel `contextmenu`, and never require double-click, drag or hold. Rationale: some 4 year olds click mostly with the right button, and about half had trouble with double-click and drag ([01](01-design-principles.md)).
6. **Keep the native cursor.** Show the OS cursor (optional custom image of 64 px, never over 128), draw a halo at the true hit radius, and make the character a trailing buddy that never owns the hit test; attract demos move that character, not a drawn hand. Rationale: a canvas cursor lags on a weak GPU, and only 50% of children read a cartoon hand as their own ([01](01-design-principles.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
7. **Hover highlights, click launches, outside games.** Hub tiles, name bubbles and on-screen keys highlight on hover and act only on click or a key while hovered. Rationale: a drifting cursor would otherwise start games ([01](01-design-principles.md), [03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
8. **Add input hardening.** `preventDefault` on Backspace, Tab, Space, arrows and F-keys; cancel Ctrl-wheel and Ctrl plus, minus and 0; `overscroll-behavior: none`; skip `event.repeat`; manifest `display_override: ["fullscreen"]` over `standalone`, scripted `requestFullscreen()` and a `navigator.keyboard.lock()` attempt; no `beforeunload` dialog; README steps to turn off Sticky Keys and touchpad gestures. Rationale: any key can leave or break the page, and Alt+F4, Ctrl+W and the Windows key cannot be blocked from a page ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
9. **Add a physical-size calibration.** Add `uiScale` to `config.json`, measure 96 CSS px on the laptop with a ruler, check its Windows scaling setting before laying out name entry, and never letterbox a fixed stage. Rationale: 96 CSS px is 17 to 24 mm depending on panel, and at 150% scaling the name screen does not fit at the floor ([01](01-design-principles.md), [03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).

### Pop game

10. **Remove decoys.** Drop "gentle decoys" and any "pop only X" phase from v1; every bubble is a valid pop, and a matching "color of the moment" may only add sparkle. Rationale: accuracy fell from about 96% to 68% when 4 and 5 year olds mixed two rules ([01](01-design-principles.md), [02](02-pop-game-feel.md)).
11. **Replace the tier knobs with the tier table.** Tiers change rise speed (45 to 60, 70 to 100, 110 to 140 CSS px/s), live cap (2 to 4, 4 to 6, 6 to 8), spacing (1.5, 1.2, 1.0 diameters) and round length (60, 75, 90 s); drawn size stays near 128 to 144 px; bubbles never time out; tier 1 escapes recycle and are not misses. Rationale: under-5s should not face timed reactions, and size barely needs to change ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md)).
12. **Fix one hit-padding rule.** Hit radius = drawn radius x 1.25, 1.15, 1.10 by tier, set at round start, hit diameter never under 96 px. Rationale: sections 01, 02 and 06 gave three different rules; 02's is the game-specific one ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [README](README.md#where-the-sections-disagree)).
13. **Specify the adaptive controller.** Replace "rolling hit rate and time-to-hit" with: motor pop rate (popped over spawned, first 3 to 5 s and idle stretches of 3 s or more excluded) targets 85%; tier down under 75% for a round; tier up at 90% or more over 30+ bubbles two rounds running; one step per round boundary; each session starts one step below the stored tier; in-round, only the spawn gap and one drift-toward-cursor assist move. A separate content estimator sets the count range (1 to 5 up to 10) on 60 / 65 to 80 / 80% bands. Rationale: two loops on one knob spiral, and a clumsy hand should not shrink the counting range ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
14. **Make a new name's first round untimed.** It ends after about 8 pops with 3 stars guaranteed, and the first bubble of every round spawns within about 250 px of the pointer. Rationale: Pikmin holds the clock until the first success, and a hard first round may end play for an unsupervised child ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
15. **Shape the round.** 10 s warm-up with one bubble at a time, 3 to 4 waves of 8 to 12 s with 4 to 6 s breathers, then a generous finale shower. Rationale: constant intensity tires players; the Left 4 Dead pacing cycle scales down to 75 s ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md)).
16. **Define the counting.** No numerals on bubbles; each pop flies a theme icon into a jar of 10; the numeral appears beside matching dots at 3, 5 and 10; the round-end tally lands item by item; frame it as a goal ("the whale wants 10 fish"); derive quotas from the spawn schedule and assert completability in dev builds. Rationale: bubble apps scored 0 on active learning unless counting lives in the action ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md)).
17. **Give pitch to the count.** Pop *n* plays step *n* of a major pentatonic ladder; bubble size sets the noise-body filter; combo drives music layers; pops play on the input frame and are never quantized to the beat. Rationale: three systems claimed pitch, and rising notes make the count audible without voice ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).
18. **Set combo values.** 2.5 s chain window, milestones at 3, 6 and 10, effects capped at 10, shown as pips or an arc and never a multiplier, and a broken chain shrinks silently. Rationale: adult-tuned windows break constantly for these children ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).
19. **Set juice limits.** No screen shake and no hit-stop on pops; squash 80 to 120 ms; 6 to 10 particles per pop, about 150 live at most; no `shadowBlur` in the loop; at most 3 flashes per second and no flashing region over 21,824 CSS px squared. Rationale: shake moves the target, hit-stop reads as stutter on a weak GPU, and the stricter flash figure needs no measurement ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).
20. **Add one surprise per round.** About 1 in 15 spawns is a silly variant (a bubble with a face, a rubber duck) with a bigger reaction and no extra score. Rationale: Mario Wonder puts a surprise in every course and Toca a quirk in every scene ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).
21. **Define stars.** 1 star for finishing, 2 for beating the child's own median longest combo over recent rounds, 3 for that plus a completed 10-count; aim for 60 to 70% of rounds at 3 stars; earned stars fill in with no empty slots. Rationale: the controller holds pop rate near 85%, so pop-rate stars would never vary ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).
22. **Make misses silent or soft.** Silence or a quiet bloop, never a falling tone or buzzer; the mascot never droops. Rationale: Big Brain Academy reworked its wrong sound because children are sensitive to it ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).

### Round end, rewards and the break

23. **Specify the celebration and rest screen.** About 4 s, skippable by any input from the first frame, 4 variants rising to 6 with no back-to-back repeat, then a still screen with Again and Home of equal size, brightness and motion, no auto-advance, and a 250 to 400 ms guard so mashing through the celebration does not trigger either. Rationale: a child quit games to avoid a repeated dance, and autoplay removes the chance to stop ([02](02-pop-game-feel.md), [04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).
24. **Specify the sticker.** One per finished round regardless of stars, drawn without replacement from the current page, the child picks one of two wrapped gifts, it flies into the book automatically; v1 book is 3 pages of 8; when full, extras upgrade owned stickers; each sticker plays a 2 to 4 s cause-and-effect animation when clicked; silhouettes appear only inside the book. Rationale: expected rewards lower later interest, and causally rich rewards beat stickers on persistence ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).
25. **Pin down the break nudge.** Count visible play time only; trigger at the next round boundary, never mid-round; dusk colors, music 20 to 30% slower without percussion, a content yawn; at the nudge only, a larger moon or home icon is allowed while replay stays visible at 96 px or more. Rationale: no countdowns or loss, and asymmetry toward stopping is the allowed direction ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
26. **Add the red lines and PR checklist to `docs/design.md`.** Copy the ten red lines and the parasocial, time, navigation, lure and absence checks into the design doc and the PR template. Rationale: legal compliance does not enforce these, so the hub has to ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).
27. **Constrain the "design for" items.** Unlocks come from page and book completion, never star thresholds; all game tiles stay open; the future pet only grows, from rounds played, never from the clock, and never shows hunger, sadness or decay. Rationale: star gates let the hidden tier split the siblings' collections, and decaying pets are a documented pressure pattern ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).

### Name entry

28. **Change the name cap from 12 to 10.** Ten 96 px tiles with 16 px gaps fill 1104 px; the 11th press gives a soft bounce. Rationale: 12 letters do not fit at the 96 px floor ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
29. **Specify key handling.** One fixed pentatonic note per letter, pre-rendered at boot; read `event.key` uppercased; skip `event.repeat`; merge presses within 30 ms; at most about 8 sounds per second, extra presses animate silently; digits 1 to 9 burst that many sparkles; any other key sparkles; nothing is ever an error. Rationale: fixed notes make each name a stable tune, and mashing is normal at this age ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
30. **Specify commit.** A pulsing play arrow appears after the first letter; it, Enter or Space commits; the letters bounce in order with the name tune and the letter count shows as a numeral beside dots. Rationale: a "go play" button, not a confirm dialog ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
31. **Set the on-screen keyboard.** ABC order in rows of 9, 9 and 8 by default, QWERTY as a JSON option; light the matching key on every physical press; invisible padding fills the gaps between keys. Rationale: ABC suits children who know the alphabet, and the lit key links the two keyboards without text ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
32. **Specify the returning-child picker.** Always require one click, never auto-resume; each bubble shows the initial, a critter and a color assigned at creation and forced to differ from existing profiles (not a hash of the name); bubbles about 150 px; a typed letter prefix-filters; ignore a second click within 400 ms; keep at most about 5 profiles. Rationale: with siblings, auto-resume credits the wrong child half the time, and a hash can give both the same avatar ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
33. **Specify name matching and allow seeded profiles.** Normalize with NFKC, lowercase, strip non-letters and collapse runs of 3 or more; exact match is the same child; an edit-distance-1 "is this you?" bubble only for names of 4+ letters, never an automatic merge; `config.json` may seed profiles with aliases and ships empty, so no names are in code. Rationale: Mia, Max and Mya sit within one or two edits, and a child who cannot spell needs a bubble from day one ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
34. **Add a no-typing path.** A big one-click "surprise animal" bubble starts play for a child who cannot type a name. Rationale: the 4 year old may not spell their name, and play should start without menu layers ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
35. **Name the typeface.** Self-host Andika (unmodified files, or a renamed subset with `OFL.txt`), uppercase only, glyphs 56 px or more, baked into an atlas after `document.fonts.load()` resolves. Rationale: single-story a and g, distinct I, l and 1, and a canvas drawn before the font loads keeps the fallback face ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).

### Audio

36. **Specify the unlock.** Capture-phase listeners on `pointerdown`, `pointerup`, `click`, `keydown` and `keyup` from boot; the first event resumes the context and plays its own sound; sounds made while suspended are dropped, not queued; re-arm whenever `statechange` leaves `running`; the mute icon has three states (on, muted, waiting). Rationale: queued notes fire at once on resume, and hover never counts as a gesture ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
37. **Specify the audio graph.** SFX and music buses into a master gain, a `DynamicsCompressorNode` and a `WaveShaper` soft clip near -3 dBFS; voice cap 8, steal the oldest; vendor only ZzFX `buildSamples` at `ctx.sampleRate`; no Tone.js; mute ramps gain rather than suspending. Rationale: the compressor alone lets transients overshoot, and stock ZzFX creates its own context that never resumes ([05](05-art-audio-direction.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
38. **Synthesize the music.** 8-bar phrases in one key shuffled with no immediate repeat, a lookahead scheduler on the audio clock, layers added on bar boundaries as the combo grows, ducking while a count shows. Rationale: decoded stereo loops cost 384 KB per second, and phrase shuffling holds up over long play ([05](05-art-audio-direction.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
39. **Calibrate loudness.** Add `masterTrimDb` to `config.json`, set per laptop with a meter to 70 to 75 dB(A) at 50 cm; start music about 12 dB under SFX peaks; an adult plays 30 minutes before ship and reworks any grating sound. Rationale: the WHO-ITU child figure is 75 dB(A), and the device will not protect the child ([05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).

### Art

40. **Limit AI art to static stills.** Draw everything that animates or reacts in code (mascot, bubbles, stars, confetti, UI); use codex-image-gen only for stickers, backgrounds and skin stills; a sharp build script trims, alpha-bleeds, resizes, outlines and palette-snaps; a human reviews every sprite; log prompt, model and date per asset; never let the model draw letters, numerals or counted items; Kenney CC0 packs as placeholders. Rationale: image models do not hold a recurring character steady, and one disturbing image draws 1-star reviews ([05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).
41. **Add `docs/style-bible.md`.** Fix corner radius, outline weight, palette tokens and tints, mascot and eye spec, shadow rule and icon set, plus one line per game naming its medium. Rationale: Toca and Bluey keep varied work consistent through a few fixed anchors ([05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).
42. **Set bubble and color rules.** Solid bubbles with a tinted rim and flat highlight, not translucent; 3:1 contrast against the background checked by a script; full chroma only on small interactive things; one highlight color for "touch me"; every color paired with a shape; prototypical hues for colors the child learns to name; check in grayscale. Rationale: a pale bubble vanishes on a pale sea on a weak panel, and about 8% of boys have a color vision deficiency ([01](01-design-principles.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).
43. **Specify the mascot.** 1:2 head to body, solid dot eyes, four states (idle smile, delight, curious, yawn), feeling shown through squash and bounce, never sad or hurt, readable as a 48 px silhouette; offer three or more non-gendered characters to pick from later. Rationale: children rated baby-like faces cuter, and missed subtle facial feedback ([05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).
44. **Read reduced motion.** Read `prefers-reduced-motion` into a `motionScale`, with a developer override in `config.json`, turning zoom and large pulses into fades. Rationale: attract mode and idle hub motion run past 5 s unprompted ([01](01-design-principles.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).

### Performance, PWA and verification

45. **Change the frame-time check.** Keep "under 12 ms at 4x CPU throttling" as a dev-machine smoke test, and add the authoritative check on the children's laptop in Chrome and Edge, plugged in and on battery, at its real Windows scaling: under 1% dropped frames over 60 s in the worst scene and p95 frame time at or under 16.7 ms. Rationale: CPU throttling does not model a weak GPU's fill rate ([06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
46. **Add a backing-pixel budget.** Cap backing pixels near 1.5 M and step an invisible resolution scale of 1.0, 0.85, 0.7 when p95 frame time stays over about 20 ms for 2 s, with hysteresis; never call `getImageData` on the game canvas; no per-frame `fillText`. Rationale: a 1366x768 canvas at DPR 1.5 is 2.36 M pixels, and one readback can drop the canvas off the GPU for good ([06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
47. **Define the payload number.** Keep under 5 MB for v1 and define it as the full precache, since offline play needs every file; set about 15 MB as the ceiling as games are added; add `scripts/check-precache.mjs` so CI fails when a `public/` file is missing from the service worker manifest. Rationale: a 4 year old cannot diagnose a missing sound offline ([06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
48. **Harden storage and updates.** Prefix every storage key and cache name (`cwl.v1.`), never call `localStorage.clear()`, call `navigator.storage.persist()` after the first finished round, save on every award and on `visibilitychange`, apply updates with `updateSW(true)` on the hub after a celebration, and wrap each lazy game `load()` in a reload-once catch. Rationale: GitHub project sites share one origin and one quota, and an update can strand old code requesting a removed chunk ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md), [04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
49. **Add verification steps.** Offline test against `vite preview` with the real base path (wait for the worker, go offline, normal reload, never shift-reload, play a full round); deploy twice with a round open and confirm the round finishes; `chrome://gpu` shows hardware canvas; `--disable-gpu` holds about 30 fps; flat heap over 60 s; no clipping at 20+ clicks per second; grayscale and color-vision emulation pass; rest-screen icons measure equal. Rationale: these are the failure modes the sections found ([01](01-design-principles.md), [05](05-art-audio-direction.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
50. **Add dev hooks behind `?debug`.** Forced tier, RNG seed, time scale, frame-time overlay, resolution-scale key, and input record and replay. Rationale: a fixed step plus a seeded RNG gives repeatable perf runs without unit tests ([06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
51. **Keep a playtest log off by default.** `playtestLog: false` in `config.json`; round-relative times only, profile slot instead of name, no daily totals, never leaves the device, wiped on export or after 14 days, stored in a preallocated ring. Rationale: the owner needs observation data, and the ethics rules forbid tracking play time ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
52. **Add the reward-off test to the handover.** Note that `config.json` can switch stickers off for a week to compare unprompted game starts. Rationale: a large drop when stickers stop is the warning sign that rewards replaced interest ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).

## Fixed decisions the research argues against

Flagged only. The owner decides.

- **No voice.** Without voice the hub can teach letter shapes but not letter names or sounds, which is what the evidence ties to letter knowledge; 26 short letter-name clips would be the highest-value audio to add ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
- **A reflex pop game as the first game.** NN/g says designs for under-5s should not need quick reactions to a visual stimulus, and bubble apps scored 0 on active learning; the research makes it work only with an untimed tier 1 and counting built into every pop ([01](01-design-principles.md), [02](02-pop-game-feel.md)).
- **Stars and a sticker book.** Expected tangible rewards lowered children's later interest (d = -0.43), and the most trusted apps for this age (PBS KIDS, Pok Pok) ship no reward systems; the research keeps them only as gifts and recommends a reward-off week to check ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).
- **"Addictive" as a goal.** Section 04 reads it as replay from delight, mastery and a growing world, not retention loops, and asks the owner to confirm that reading in writing ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).
- **Pixel art in the art mix.** Pixel art needs integer scaling, renders unevenly at DPR 1.5 or 1.25, cannot sit beside smooth vector on one screen, and no study tests how 4 to 5 year olds read it; playtest before committing a game to it ([05](05-art-audio-direction.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
- **AI-generated sprites per game.** Image models do not keep a recurring character consistent, so AI art fits stills but not animated characters or anything the child must recognize repeatedly ([05](05-art-audio-direction.md)).
- **One profile per typed name.** Misspellings and partial names would create stray profiles and split a child's stickers; the research prefers a click-to-pick list, seeded profiles with aliases, and suggestions without automatic merges ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md)).
- **A single nudge at about 20 minutes.** The Canadian Paediatric Society advises about 1 hour or less per day, which one nudge per session cannot reflect; an optional per-profile re-yawn after a daily threshold is an open question ([04](04-rewards-progression-ethics.md)).
- **Unsupervised play in a browser.** Alt+F4, Ctrl+W, the Windows key and Alt+Tab cannot be blocked from a page; the installed PWA in fullscreen plus one-time OS setup reduces but does not remove the risk ([03](03-name-entry-typing.md), [06](06-tech-architecture-perf.md)).
- **A 96 CSS px target floor.** On an 11.6 inch or 1080p panel it measures 17 to 18 mm, under the 2 cm minimum for young children; the floor holds only with a measured `uiScale` ([01](01-design-principles.md)).
